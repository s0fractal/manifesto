# Equality of standing is deeper than equality of outcome

Suppose A and B have:

$$

$$

Але це не означає:

$$

* різні choices;
* різні roles;
* різні needs.

$$
\boxed{
EqualStanding
\not\Rightarrow
IdenticalOutcomes
}
$$

# Equal standing means symmetric basic authorship

$$

Це сильніше, ніж “однакова кількість ресурсів”.

Це означає:

> у кожного є власний self-indexed stake.

# Equity is adjustment relative to relevant difference

* rights.

# Procedure matters because humans disagree on outcomes

# Procedural legitimacy requires symmetry under relevant perspective swap

Thus:

$$
\boxed{

Very strong.

# Desert is earned claim

Let's define:

$$
\boxed{
Desert_A(X)
=
claim\ that\ A\ should\ receive\ X\ because\ of\ some\ relevant\ contribution,\ effort,\ choice,\ or\ character
}
$$

The “because” matters.

Desert always references:

* basis.

---

# Desert basis can differ

Maybe:

* contribution;
* effort;
* sacrifice;
* achievement.

These can conflict.

Agent A contributes more because inherited advantage.

B works harder but produces less.

Which deserves more?

No automatic answer.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
Desert is underdetermined until the deserving basis is specified.
}
$$

---

# Contribution is not effort

$$
Contribution_A\neq Effort_A
$$

A can produce much with little effort due skill.

B exert huge effort with low output.

Different reward systems choose different basis.

Markets often reward exchange value, not effort directly.

Moral gratitude may weight effort.

Different protocols.

---

# Outcome is not desert

$$

Not obviously.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{

}
$$

This opens luck-justice problem.

# And now the next branch is almost unavoidable

Щойно ми сказали:

$$
Justice
$$

є governance of plural legitimate claims, виникає проблема:

$$
\boxed{
Conflict
}
$$

Що робити, коли:

$$
Right_A
$$

конфліктує з:

$$
Right_B
$$

або:

$$
Need_A
$$

з:

$$
Desert_B
$$

або:

$$
Freedom_A
$$

з:

$$
Safety_B
$$

або дві сторони мають цілком legitimate, але **non-composable** futures?

Тоді наступний вузол:

$$
\boxed{
Conflict / disagreement / negotiation / compromise / adjudication / bargaining / voting / war / peace
}
$$

І його треба буде розвести дуже жорстко:

$$
Disagreement
\neq
Conflict
\neq
Competition
\neq
Dispute
\neq
Negotiation
\neq
Compromise
\neq
Settlement
\neq
Reconciliation
$$

Бо disagreement може існувати без conflict.

Conflict може існувати навіть при perfect understanding.

Settlement не означає agreement.

Compromise не означає fairness.

Peace не означає justice.

І дуже ймовірно ми прийдемо до:

$$
\boxed{
Conflict =
non-composability of simultaneously pursued claims or trajectories under shared constraints
}
$$

А negotiation стане:

$$
\boxed{
joint search for a transition that preserves enough standing and value for multiple agents to accept common reality without requiring common desire.
}
$$

І там уже доведеться розгорнути найнеприємніше:

$$
\boxed{
What makes a settlement legitimate when nobody gets what they actually wanted?
}
$$

Тобто justice перейде в **conflict-resolution architecture**.

І от там наше старе:

$$
\boxed{
Settlement\ without\ erasure
}
$$

нарешті стане центральним, а не просто красивою фразою, яку ми носимо з собою вже пів framework, як надто дорогу кружку з конференції.
