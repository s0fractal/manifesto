# 114. The **Narrative Principle**

$$
\boxed{
Narratives are temporal compressions that organize events into intelligible trajectories; they should be evaluated for selection, causality, perspective, omission, and downstream action—not merely for internal coherence.
}
$$

---

# 115. The **Fact/Narrative Principle**

$$
\boxed{
Facts constrain narratives without uniquely determining them; multiple interpretations may fit the same evidence, but not every interpretation is equally compatible with the evidence.
}
$$

Central.

---

# 116. The **Narrative-Coherence Principle**

$$
\boxed{
A story's smoothness, emotional force, or causal neatness should not be treated as evidence proportional to its coherence.
}
$$

---

# 117. The **Fiction Principle**

$$
\boxed{
Fiction can be epistemically and morally valuable as counterfactual simulation without being confused with literal testimony about actual events or populations.
}
$$

---

# 118. The **Myth Principle**

$$
\boxed{
Culturally foundational narratives should be analyzed separately for historical accuracy, symbolic function, identity formation, and institutional consequence rather than reduced to a binary true/false label.
}
$$

---

# 119. The **Vision Principle**

$$
\boxed{
A vision should orient search and commitment without being promoted into either a complete implementation plan or an inevitable forecast.
}
$$

---

# 120. The **Scenario Principle**

$$
\boxed{
Use imagined futures to test robustness and expose assumptions, but keep possibility, plausibility, probability, desirability, and prediction explicitly separate.
}
$$

Very important.

# 121. The **Ideology Principle**

$$
\boxed{

Excellent.

# 122. The **Anti-Self-Sealing Principle**
