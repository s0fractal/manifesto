What this honestly is — and isn't

This verifies identity: that a term's bytes hash to a given address. It recomputes seven canonical node addresses from their bytes and compares them to pinned values — that is all, and it is enough to be worth doing. It is the ts-sigma v0 layer: it runs no evaluator, so there is no budget, no exit and no receipt here. It therefore settles nothing — a settlement, in this stack's sense, is a verdict carried by two receipts with their exits, under a named profile with an admitted domain. An earlier version of this page said it showed that "two constructions reaching the same normal form share one address (equality-by-address)"; this page computes no normal forms, and equal addresses prove identity of what came back rather than equality of any inputs (errata). And it verifies the derivation, not the meaning: that these bytes carry the intended concept is a separate, human obligation. The honest claim is small and exact — which is the whole point.
